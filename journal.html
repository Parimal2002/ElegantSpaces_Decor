DESIGN NOTES / 01

A softer approach to minimalism

Start with a restrained palette, then add depth through wood, linen, and different textures. Keep the pieces that make everyday life comfortable. A room can be simple and still feel warm.

DESIGN NOTES / 02

Three ways to make a bedroom feel calmer

Keep bedside surfaces clear, layer gentle lighting, and choose textiles in complementary tones. Before buying new furniture, check that you can move easily around your bed.

DESIGN NOTES / 03

Plan your kitchen around your routine

Think about how you prepare, cook, and clean. Put frequently used items within easy reach and plan task lighting for worktops. Practical planning creates a kitchen that feels effortless.
